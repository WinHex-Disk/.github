## First Look at WinHex

WinHex is for people who keep a disk image they own and want one named hex desk, not unnamed sector dumps. It gives you a disk you own, own files, and a short path back to a sector view you already store. It does not replace the rest of your PC. It sits beside the image you already keep.

Work stays on files you own. Extra images wait for their own title.
